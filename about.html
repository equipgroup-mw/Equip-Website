Our story

Equip Group was founded on the belief that small businesses deserve the right support to thrive. Our founder, Rollins Chitika, envisioned walking alongside entrepreneurs, offering the guidance, networks, and opportunities he wished more businesses had.

In 2019, that vision took shape. With just a laptop and determination, Rollins began supporting entrepreneurs facing challenges. What started as a one-person effort evolved into a management consulting firm grounded in empathy, action, and results.

From the start, our goal has been to deliver practical solutions that empower organizations for the long term. Today, Equip Group remains true to its purpose: helping businesses start, build, and grow, with every client and success story reaffirming why we continue.
